About Vivarium Lab

Our group builds comprehensive models that integrate diverse biological data sources across scales. Rather than isolated representational systems, we unify multi-level mechanisms into predictive simulations that can interpret experiments, generate hypotheses, and guide engineering.

Research

Compositional systems biology for robust predictions

We are developing frameworks that let models grow by assembling smaller, reusable components. This approach supports flexible model reuse across domains such as microbial physiology, cell communities, synthetic cells, and emergent behavior in tissues.
