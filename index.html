Curriculum

What you will learn

Week 1: Linux basics and command line workflows.
Week 2: Linux, HPC, and Bash scripting for automation.
Week 3: Python for biological data analysis and visualization.
Week 4: R programming, statistics, and ggplot2 visualization.
Week 5: RNA-seq and gene expression analysis.
Week 6: Functional analysis with GO and pathway enrichment.
Week 7: Research workflows, GitHub, and reproducibility.
Week 8: Final project with real dataset and report.
